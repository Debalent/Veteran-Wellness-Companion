# Incident Response Process

Date: 2026-07-04
Scope: Security incident lifecycle for the Veteran Wellness Companion platform.

## Severity Levels

| Severity | Description | Target Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sev 1 | Confirmed breach, data compromise, or critical service outage | Immediate triage, command bridge within 15 minutes |
| Sev 2 | High-risk event with material impact potential | Triage within 30 minutes |
| Sev 3 | Moderate-risk event, contained impact | Triage within 4 hours |
| Sev 4 | Low-risk event or suspicious activity | Triage within 1 business day |

## Response Lifecycle

1. Detect: Event identified through alerting, monitoring, or report.
2. Triage: Validate signal, classify severity, assign incident lead.
3. Contain: Isolate affected components and block further impact.
4. Eradicate: Remove threat vector and patch root cause.
5. Recover: Restore services and validate system integrity.
6. Post-Incident: Complete after-action report and corrective actions.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- Incident Commander: Owns overall response coordination.
- Security Lead: Directs investigation and containment actions.
- Engineering Lead: Executes technical remediation.
- Communications Lead: Manages internal/external communications.
- Compliance Lead: Coordinates documentation and reporting requirements.

## Communication Matrix

- Internal status updates every 30 minutes for Sev 1 and Sev 2 incidents.
- Stakeholder notification windows defined by severity and impact.
- Executive summary issued at containment and closure milestones.

## Minimum Incident Record

- Incident ID and timeline.
- Detection source and affected systems.
- Impact assessment (data, users, operations).
- Containment and remediation actions.
- Validation evidence for recovery.
- Root cause and corrective action plan.

## Tabletop and Readiness Cadence

- Quarterly incident response tabletop exercise.
- Semiannual runbook review and update.
- Corrective actions tracked to closure with owners and due dates.

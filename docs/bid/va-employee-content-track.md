# VA Employee Content Track Plan

Date: 2026-07-04
Purpose: Define workforce wellness scope for VA employees alongside veteran-facing features.

## 1. Audience Segmentation

- Veterans: wellness support, resilience, safety planning, self-monitoring
- VA Employees (clinical and non-clinical): burnout mitigation, recovery habits, transition support
- Supervisors/Leads (optional reporting path): aggregate, consent-based program indicators

## 2. Content Pillars for VA Employees

### Burnout Prevention

- Early signs of occupational burnout
- Boundary setting and cognitive load management
- Short, repeatable decompression practices

### Shift-Transition Recovery

- Pre-shift preparation routines
- Post-shift decompression and sleep hygiene
- Rapid reset protocols for high-intensity roles

### Resiliency and Retention Support

- Micro-learning modules for stress adaptation
- Goal-based habit plans for recovery consistency
- Peer-support pathways and referral guidance

## 3. Product Requirements

- Role-aware onboarding path for VA employees
- Dedicated VA employee dashboard content lane
- Optional reminders for shift-transition routines
- Employee-safe language with non-diagnostic framing
- Crisis resources visible with clear escalation guidance

## 4. Outcome Measures

| Metric | Definition | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module completion rate | % of assigned content completed | Weekly |
| Active usage rate | % of users active in last 7/30 days | Weekly/Monthly |
| Routine adherence | Completion rate of shift-transition routines | Weekly |
| Self-reported stress trend | Mean trend by consented cohorts | Monthly |
| Retention proxy | Repeat engagement after 30/60/90 days | Monthly |

## 5. Delivery Phases

- Phase A: Baseline content library and role routing
- Phase B: Reminders and routine adherence tracking
- Phase C: Supervisor-facing aggregate insights (de-identified, consent-based)

## 6. Governance and Safeguards

- No diagnosis, prediction, or clinical triage claims
- Privacy-by-design controls and explicit consent boundaries
- De-identified analytics for organizational reporting
- Clear escalation path to licensed support and crisis services
